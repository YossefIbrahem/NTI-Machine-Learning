About the model
  • Logistic Regression (C = 0.1) in a scikit-learn Pipeline, chosen from 11 classification models compared with stratified 5-fold cross-validation.
  • Test accuracy 99.75 %, F1 (macro) 0.992, ROC-AUC 0.998; it missed no diabetic patient on the test set.
  • Feature selection showed that only HbA1c and blood sugar matter; the two "above the limit" flags are engineered features.
  • The trained model runs entirely in your browser and gives exactly the same probabilities as the Python model.
